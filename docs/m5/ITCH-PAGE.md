## One-line statement (jam requirement: what it simulates, who it is for)
Jar Garden simulates a sealed terrarium ecosystem (humidity, fresh air, light and day/night, moss growth, mould, a small food web) for players who enjoy gentle, readable systems and cozy games.

## Description

**A tiny living world in a jar.** Moss, ferns, mushrooms, snails, springtails and fireflies keep each other alive, or don't. Your neighbours on the windowsill send you little notes. Help their wishes come true and they will send you postcards of your jar.

**How it fits SIMULATION**

Everything in the jar is one connected system, and every number has a visible cause:
- **Air and humidity.** Moss and ferns make fresh air in the light. Animals use it up. An open lid swaps the jar's air with the room: fresh air rushes in, but the jar dries out. A closed jar keeps its damp, and too much damp fogs the glass.
- **Light and time.** The sun moves across the day. The lamp lets moss grow at night, but fireflies hate it.
- **Soil.** Every patch of soil has its own moss, leftovers and mould. Moss spreads to its neighbours. Leftovers rot into mould when the jar is wet and still. Mould smothers moss.
- **A small food web:**
  - snails eat leftovers, then moss
  - springtails eat mould
  - mushrooms clean up leftovers and keep mould away
  - fireflies need a moss carpet, damp air and darkness to glow
- **Life and death.** Happy creatures have babies. Sad ones lose health. Bodies become leftovers, and leftovers feed the mould.
- **Seeds.** Every midnight, each happy species gives you a seed. You can watch the seeds fly out of the creatures that earned them. Seeds buy new life.

A neglected jar slowly falls apart. A balanced one can look after itself with the lid closed. Tap any creature to see how it feels and why.

**Goal**

Six neighbour notes guide you from your first glowing firefly to a firefly party:
- a moss carpet
- a light show
- cleaning up a mould bloom
- a jar that looks after itself

Finish all six for the party and a collage of your postcards, then keep playing as long as you like. If every animal dies, the jar goes quiet. Rewind to the start of that note and try again.

**How to play** (mouse or touch)
- **Pick, then place:** pick a creature in the shop, then tap inside the jar to place it. Tap outside the jar or press Esc to cancel.
- **Open lid / Close lid:** fresh air in, damp out.
- **Lamp:** light at night. Moss grows, fireflies sulk.
- **Mist:** +10 humidity.
- **Speed** (unlocks after the first note): 1x, 2x, 4x or pause. Space also cycles it.
- **Inspect:** tap a creature to read its mood. The bubbles above creatures show what they need. The panel shows the bars with their causes (▲ / ▼), the last 15 days, and the next payday.
- **Mute:** the speaker icon (top right).

A day lasts 60 seconds at 1x. A full playthrough takes about 5–10 minutes.
